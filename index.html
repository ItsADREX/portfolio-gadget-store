FAQ

Questions, answered.

Are your phones and laptops original? +

Yes. Every new device is sealed in the factory box with original accessories. We provide IMEI/serial confirmation and you can verify directly with the manufacturer before payment.

How does WhatsApp ordering work? +

Tap the WhatsApp button on any product. We confirm availability, share photos and final pricing, take your delivery address, then dispatch. You can pay on delivery in Lagos, Abuja and Port Harcourt.

What does "certified pre-owned" mean? +

Each device passes a 28-point inspection: battery health (must be 85%+), screen, cameras, ports, IMEI clean status, and original-parts verification. You get a condition report and a 30-day warranty.

Do you offer installments? +

Yes — 3 and 6-month plans available on devices above ₦400,000 through our partner financiers. We also accept bank transfer, card, and cash.

Do you ship outside Lagos? +

Yes — nationwide via DHL, GIG and Topship. Delivery is free on orders above ₦500,000. Same-day in Lagos, 24–48h to other states.
